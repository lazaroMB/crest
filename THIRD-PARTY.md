# Third-party software

crest is licensed under the [GNU General Public License v3.0 or later](LICENSE). It is built on third-party software that keeps its own license:

- **Rust crates.** The binary links the crates listed in `Cargo.lock`, almost all under MIT and/or Apache-2.0 (a few under BSD, ISC, Zlib, Unicode-3.0, CC0 or MPL-2.0). Each crate's license is in its `Cargo.toml` and source; `cargo metadata --format-version 1` lists them all.
- **three.js r160** (MIT, © 2010-2023 three.js authors), bundled unmodified in [`crates/adapters/export/vendor/three/0.160.0/`](crates/adapters/export/vendor/three/0.160.0/) with its [license](crates/adapters/export/vendor/three/0.160.0/LICENSE), and inlined into the `board.html` files crest exports.
- **Example color themes** in [`examples/themes/`](examples/themes/) (copied by the installers into the config folder) take their colors from Vim and Neovim colorschemes. Each file names its source commit and keeps the upstream copyright notice: [catppuccin/nvim](https://github.com/catppuccin/nvim) (MIT, © 2021 Catppuccin), [folke/tokyonight.nvim](https://github.com/folke/tokyonight.nvim) (Apache-2.0, Folke Lemaitre), [rebelot/kanagawa.nvim](https://github.com/rebelot/kanagawa.nvim) (MIT, © 2021 Tommaso Laurenzi), [rose-pine/neovim](https://github.com/rose-pine/neovim) (MIT, © 2023 Rosé Pine), [ellisonleao/gruvbox.nvim](https://github.com/ellisonleao/gruvbox.nvim) (MIT, © 2021 NpX; palette of [morhetz/gruvbox](https://github.com/morhetz/gruvbox), MIT/X11), [EdenEast/nightfox.nvim](https://github.com/EdenEast/nightfox.nvim) (MIT, © 2021 James Simpson), [navarasu/onedark.nvim](https://github.com/navarasu/onedark.nvim) (MIT, © 2021 Navarasu), [dracula/vim](https://github.com/dracula/vim) (MIT, © 2016 Dracula Theme), [nordtheme/vim](https://github.com/nordtheme/vim) (MIT, © 2016-present Sven Greb) and [sainnhe/everforest](https://github.com/sainnhe/everforest) (MIT, © 2019 sainnhe).
